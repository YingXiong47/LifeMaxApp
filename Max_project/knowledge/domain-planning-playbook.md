# LifeMax Domain Planning Playbook

Use this document as static file-search knowledge for the strategy and domain planning agents.

## Global rules

- Plans must survive real schedules.
- Every domain plan must include measurable weekly actions.
- If baseline data is incomplete, the first task should improve the baseline.
- Do not give three ambitious tasks to someone with very low time or energy.

## Strategy rules

Phase 1:

- reduce instability
- create control
- protect energy

Phase 2:

- build leverage
- create visible progress
- strengthen one or two priority domains

Phase 3:

- compound only after adherence is proven
- avoid scaling chaos

## Domain plan requirements

Each domain plan must include:

- targetOutcome
- currentBaseline
- actionItems
- kpiMetrics
- reviewPeriod
- confidenceScore
- riskFlags

## KPI quality bar

Good KPI examples:

- wake time within 30 minutes on 6 of 7 days
- 4 focus blocks weekly
- 1 weekly money review
- 2 outreach actions this month

Bad KPI examples:

- improve consistency
- feel better
- look sharper
- think strategically

## Deadline quality bar

Good deadlines:

- 5 days
- 7 days
- weekly
- 14 days

Bad deadlines:

- soon
- ongoing
- when possible
